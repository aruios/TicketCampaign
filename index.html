Mission

Turn a hundred solo bookings into one group fare.

Every diaspora family flying home for the same festival is, to the airline, a hundred strangers paying peak price alone. Kootam pools that same demand into a single group booking of 10 to 50 seats — the size airlines already discount for — and passes the group rate back to everyone who pledged.

Vision

Nobody skips Pongal because of the airfare.

We want a Tamil Sangam, a temple, or a student association in any city to open the app, see enough neighbors want the same dates, and unlock a fare that makes going home for the festival an easy yes — not a $1,800 sacrifice.

Why the dates matter

The three weeks everyone needs a seat are the three weeks fares spike hardest.

Diwali in November, then Christmas straight into Pongal in mid-January — LAX–MAA fares in that window regularly run well past what the same trip costs in March. Kootam's group-fare pool exists specifically for this stretch of the calendar.

How it works

Four stubs, one trip home.

01

Vote

Say which route and month you'd fly — this is what tells us a campaign is worth opening.

02

Pledge

Put down a refundable deposit to claim a seat at the current tier price. No card hold — a real deposit, held in escrow.

03

Unlock

Hit the seat target before the deadline and the group fare locks in with the airline. Miss it, and every deposit is refunded automatically.

04

Fly

Passenger names go to the host travel agency, tickets get issued, and your PNR lands in the app.
